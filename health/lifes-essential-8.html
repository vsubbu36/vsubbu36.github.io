Life's Essential 8 — Key Points

Quick personal reference based on the American Heart Association materials provided.

1. Eat Better

  • Build meals around vegetables, fruits, whole grains, beans/legumes and nuts.
  • Prefer healthy proteins such as fish, seafood, lean poultry and low-fat dairy.
  • Drink plenty of water; unsweetened tea and coffee can also be included.
  • Limit sweetened drinks, alcohol, salty foods, fatty foods and processed meats.
  • Avoid trans fats.
  • Prefer plant oils such as olive, canola or avocado oil over coconut or palm oil.
  • Watch portions and compare nutrition labels, especially sodium, saturated/trans fat and added sugar.
  • Cooking at home can make healthy choices easier.

2. Be More Active

  • Aim for at least 150 minutes/week of moderate aerobic activity or 75 minutes/week of vigorous activity, or a combination.
  • Add muscle-strengthening activity at least 2 days/week.
  • Break up long periods of sitting with movement.
  • Increase time, distance or effort gradually.
  • Regular activity can improve mood, reduce stress and support brain health.

3. Quit Tobacco & Nicotine

  • Avoid smoking, vaping, nicotine pouches and tobacco.
  • Choose a quit date and prepare for cravings and triggers.
  • When a craving comes, delay for 10–15 minutes.
  • Water, chewing gum and deep breathing can help manage urges.
  • Consider professional support, nicotine replacement or medication when appropriate.

4. Get Healthy Sleep

  • Adults should aim for an average of 7–9 hours.
  • Keep a consistent bedtime and wake-up time.
  • Create a 20–30 minute wind-down routine.
  • Dim lights and reduce screen exposure before bed.
  • Use “Do Not Disturb” to reduce nighttime interruptions.
  • Good sleep supports mood, energy, focus, learning, memory and decision-making.

5. Manage Weight

  • Learn appropriate portions and pay attention to calories per serving.
  • Increase fiber and water intake.
  • Include protein at breakfast to help with fullness.
  • Limit fast food when possible.
  • Read serving sizes and nutrition information on food labels.
  • Exercise can help manage stress and support weight management.
  • The source notes BMI as one measure: optimal BMI less than 25; less than 18.5 is underweight.

  • Reduce sodium, saturated fat, trans fat and added sugar.
  • Use a DASH-like pattern with vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts and healthy proteins.
  • Exercise regularly and avoid nicotine.
  • Home monitoring can help track readings; diagnosis should be confirmed by a healthcare professional.

7. Control Cholesterol

  • Know and track your cholesterol numbers.
  • HDL is commonly described as “good” cholesterol; LDL as “bad” cholesterol.
  • Eat more vegetables, fruits, whole grains, beans, nuts and lean proteins such as fish and seafood.
  • Replace saturated fats such as butter and whole-fat dairy with healthier fats where appropriate.
  • Exercise can help improve cholesterol numbers.
  • Avoid smoking/nicotine.
  • If prescribed cholesterol medication, take it as directed.

  • Eat vegetables, fruits, whole grains, beans, nuts and lean proteins.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Limit sugary foods/drinks, red meat, processed meat, salty snacks and highly processed foods.
  • Exercise regularly and maintain a healthy weight.
  • Avoid smoking, vaping, nicotine pouches and tobacco.
  • Healthy blood glucose is supported by eating well, physical activity, stress management and healthy sleep.

Simple Daily Checklist

  • ☐ Mostly whole, minimally processed foods
  • ☐ Vegetables or fruit included in meals
  • ☐ Enough water
  • ☐ Physical activity / walking
  • ☐ Avoid tobacco/nicotine
  • ☐ Reasonable portions
  • ☐ Manage stress
  • ☐ 7–9 hours sleep target
Core idea: Eat better + move regularly + maintain a healthy weight + sleep well + avoid nicotine + monitor blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar.
